How this data was built

  1. Market total = 10,000 × a yearly level × a fixed monthly curve. June is always 4× January.
  2. Brand shares step by −1, 0 or +1 point a month for Valley Central, Neptune and Cornfield. Barefoot gets the remainder.
  3. Tier mix is set once per month and applied to every brand.
  4. States get a fixed slice of every brand, tier and month (Florida 16.2%), rounded to whole units.

Reading it safely

  1. Compare a period with the same months a year earlier. For periods over a year, the dashboard compares the last 12 months with the 12 before.
  2. Report small brands in share points, not % growth.
  3. Treat cells under the minimum as rounding noise.
  4. Brand share is measured against all four brands. Hiding a brand doesn't change the others' shares.
